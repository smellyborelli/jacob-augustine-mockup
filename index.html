I Love You Forever

Eight songs. Jacob Augustine's first album in a decade, released May 22, 2026 on Team Love Records. The limited 12-inch vinyl includes a lyric sheet insert.

Medulla Burning Down. Camaro. Halfway to Harlem. The General's Son. Animal Orchard. Peril. Philadelphia Lights. Midnight Drum.

Jacob Augustine sings and plays guitar. Hamilton Belk plays pedal steel, lap steel, dobro, banjo, organ and synth. Peter McLaughlin plays drums and percussion. Asher Platts plays upright and electric bass. Peter Broderick plays violin and wrote the string arrangements. Lisa/Liza sings backing vocals. Jerusha Neely plays cello.

Recorded by Peter McLaughlin, Hamilton Belk and Peter Broderick. Mixed by Noah Cole, except "Midnight Drum," mixed by Peter Broderick. Mastered by Caleb Mulkerin.

The cover photograph is the artist's mother, taken in the 1960s counter-culture era. He calls it a symbol of that ongoing movement.
